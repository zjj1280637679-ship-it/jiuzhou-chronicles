# Mobile theatre artwork

Created on 2026-10-20 with the built-in Image Gen tool, grounded in the selected second mobile UI concept and the project's imperial palace artwork. These are independent raster assets without UI, lettering or controls.

- `palace-night.webp`: snow outside a Ming palace, warm lanterns and red pillars, a kneeling official in a dark embroidered robe in the lower centre. Generated separately at1223×1286; shipped at900×946, WebP quality86, 219180bytes.
- `reader-ornament.webp`: very low contrast antique gold palace/mountain line engraving, concentrated at the bottom of an ink-black field. Generated separately at2018×779; shipped at900×347, WebP quality86, 12508bytes.

Original generated PNGs are preserved in the local task output, outside the public game assets. UI controls and text remain real HTML. `icons/` contains upstream Phosphor SVG assets with its separate MIT license and pinned source record.
